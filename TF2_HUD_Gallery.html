Published examples, not captures of your PC. Screenshots may show older versions, different resolutions, classes, themes or settings. Your hidden viewmodels and disabled 3D portrait will not be re-enabled just to match a picture. The Community HUD Fixes image is an options-menu preview.

Installer behavior

Use setup_tf2_bazzite.py v1.5.0. The old HUD choices and all performance, visual, dry-run and restore options remain in this one cumulative script. --hud keep is still the default; --hud stock removes detected custom HUDs after backup. Only one HUD is active at a time.

Center HUD and Community HUD Fixes are new pinned downloads. MaxiHud and ToonHUD are named local ZIP import options. The latter are not authenticated upstream downloads: their hashes identify the chosen bytes. The four new choices omit automatic startup hooks to protect the earlier setup; this may prevent full in-game customization menus from initializing.

HUD-only incremental updates do not rewrite your existing configuration files. Arbitrary HUD menu actions and other scripts can still change in-game settings; this is not a compatibility guarantee for every mod. Current screenshots are not proof of Bazzite runtime compatibility.

Sources and image ownership: the HUD authors and the project/catalog pages linked on each card. No fonts or HUD assets are bundled in this gallery. Prepared 19 October 2026.
